How We Reach Patients

Three Ways We Serve

Walk-In Pharmacy

Patients pick up their medicine directly at Yasmeen Asif Sajida Arshad Pharmacy, inside Creek General Hospital — a pharmacist checks every prescription before anything goes out the door.

Medical Camps

When we run a medical camp, the medicine goes with it — into neighbourhoods where getting to a hospital isn't really an option.

DAWA Healthcare (Zakat)

We work with DAWA Healthcare to turn Zakat donations into real medicine, and log every rupee from the moment it lands to the moment it's handed over.
