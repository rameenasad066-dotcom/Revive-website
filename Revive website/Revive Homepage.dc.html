What We Do

Care and training, side by side

Revive Organisation is run by medical students who believe care shouldn't wait for credentials. We deliver free medication and treatment to those who need it most, while training the next generation of clinicians in research and life-saving skills.

Our Programs

The Three Verticals

Revive Pharmacy

Free medication for those who need it most.

Revive Pharmacy runs a free dispensary and door-to-door medication drives, funded entirely by donations. Volunteer clinicians verify each prescription, and chronic-illness patients are enrolled on a repeat-supply list so treatment never stops for lack of money.
01
02

Revive Lives

Workshops that save lives.

Revive Lives trains medical students in the skills a curriculum skips — basic life support, emergency response and research methodology — through certified hands-on workshops led by practising clinicians and researchers.

Revive Camp

Medical camps for underserved communities.

Revive Camp takes screening, consultation and free medicine into villages and low-income neighbourhoods, pairing volunteer doctors with student teams so patients who would never reach a clinic are seen where they live.
