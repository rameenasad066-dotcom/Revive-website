Our Story

Two Ideas. One Mission.

Revive was founded in 2024 by Muhammad Saad bin Arshad Arain and Dania Hussain, third-year medical students at United Medical and Dental College.

During clinical rotations at Creek General Hospital, Saad kept seeing the same gap: patients could get examined and diagnosed, but many couldn't afford the medicine that came after. A diagnosis without treatment isn't complete healthcare.

Dania had a different starting point. She believed life-saving skills shouldn't be limited to healthcare professionals — that anyone should know what to do when someone collapses in front of them. Two ideas, one from the pharmacy counter and one from the classroom, became one organisation.

The name says what we set out to do — to revive someone, to give them another chance at a healthier life.
